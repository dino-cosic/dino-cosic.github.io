How I work

Four habits I bring to every engagement.

Understand the system before changing it

Legacy platforms rarely match their documentation. I reverse-engineer how a system actually behaves before changing it, because modernization starts with an accurate picture of what exists.

Own it past the merge

I take features from requirements and design through testing, deployment, and production operation. I design for the operating phase: infrastructure as code, automated and integration tests, and observability with OpenTelemetry and distributed tracing.

Make the team faster, not just myself

I’ve led a development team, reviewed implementation decisions, and mentored engineers. At university I taught and mentored more than 350 students in how real software teams deliver.

Translate between product and engineering

I work closely with Product to turn business requirements into scalable designs and delivery plans that teams can actually execute.
